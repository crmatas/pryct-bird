
# Kookaburra

**Scientific name:** Dacelo novaeguineae.  
**Family:** Alcedinidae.

## Description
It is a large bird with a brown and white body, a large head, a strong beak and distinctive blue feathers on its wings.

## Habitat
It lives in eastern Australia, mainly in forests, woodlands, parks and gardens.

## Diet
Its diet includes insects, small reptiles, snakes, mice, frogs and other small animals.

## Behaviour
It is famous for its loud call, which sounds like human laughter. It usually sits on tree branches and watches the ground before diving down to catch its prey.

## Reproduction
It usually nests in tree hollows or holes in termite mounds. Both parents help care for the chicks.
