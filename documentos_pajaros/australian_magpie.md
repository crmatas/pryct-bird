
# Australian Magpie

**Scientific name:** Gymnorhina tibicen.  
**Family:** Artamidae.

## Description
It is a medium-sized bird with distinctive black and white feathers, a strong beak and reddish-brown eyes.

## Habitat
It is commonly found in Australia, especially in grasslands, parks, gardens, forests and open areas, and the the terrace of Critina where she used to live.

## Diet
Its diet consists mainly of insects, worms, small animals and occasionally seeds.

## Behaviour
It is an intelligent and social bird. It often walks on the ground searching for food and is famous for its beautiful and complex song.

## Reproduction
It builds a nest made of sticks and branches, usually high in the branches of trees. The female lays eggs and takes care of the chicks.
