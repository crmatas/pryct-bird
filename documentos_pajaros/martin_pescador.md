
# Common Kingfisher

**Scientific name:** Alcedo atthis.  
**Family:** Alcedinidae.

## Description
It is a small bird characterized by its bright blue plumage on the back and orange feathers on its underside.

## Habitat
It lives near rivers, streams, lakes and other bodies of water where fish are abundant.

## Diet
Its diet consists mainly of small fish and other aquatic organisms.

## Behaviour
It usually sits on branches near the water and dives quickly to catch its prey.

## Reproduction
It builds its nests in tunnels excavated in earth banks near the water.
